Before We Arrive

How to prep for your OSC

A few minutes of prep on your end helps us look at the whole job and give you an accurate estimate.

What we needWhy it matters
Clear path to the stump Move vehicles, trailers, toys, hoses, and patio furniture off the route from the street to the work area.
Gate unlocked If the stump is in a fenced yard, we need in. Let us know if a gate panel has to come off.
Pets inside We'll be walking the property and don't want to startle or spook an animal that isn't expecting a stranger in the yard.
Sprinkler heads flagged If you know where your irrigation runs near the stump, mark it. We probe, but you know your yard better.
Utility locates We confirm public locates are in place. Private lines — gas to a fire pit, low-voltage lighting, invisible fence — only you know about, so tell us.
Someone reachable You don't have to be home for the OSC, but we'll need to reach you by phone with questions or your written estimate.
