What We Do

Two services, done properly

Some jobs just need the stump gone. Others need the ground ready for concrete, a fence post, or a new tree. Here's the difference.

Stump Grinding

The stump and its surface roots ground down below ground level, chips raked back into the hole and tamped level. Most single stumps are a same-visit job.

  • Ground below grade so you can mow and seed over it
  • Surface root flare taken down, not just the trunk
  • Grindings backfilled and leveled
  • Walks and drives blown clean before we pull out
Grinding Details

Stump Removal & Cleanup

Everything in grinding, plus we haul the chips off and backfill with clean topsoil. This is the one to pick when something is going in that spot.

  • Chips and wood debris hauled away, not left curbside
  • Larger roots and trunk chunks pulled out
  • Topsoil backfill, tamped so it won't sink
  • Finished level with the surrounding yard

Why Homeowners Call Us

No guesswork, no mess left behind

Plywood tracking over soft turf, shielding around siding, fences, and windows. The machine is heavy and we treat your lawn like it matters.

Utilities get checked first

We confirm locates are in place and probe the work area before the wheel turns. Grinding blind next to a gas or irrigation line is how yards get wrecked.

Hard surfaces blown down, debris handled per your scope, and a walkthrough with you at the end if you're home.

Straight answers on tricky stumps

Tight backyard access, stumps against a foundation, rock in the roots — we'll tell you what it actually takes instead of quoting low and renegotiating on site.

Multiple stumps cost less each

Setup and travel get shared across the visit. If you've got three or four, get them all done at once.
