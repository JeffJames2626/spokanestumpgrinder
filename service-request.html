Before You Start

Four things that speed this up

Put these in the Notes box on the form — each one saves a round of back-and-forth.

Diameter at ground level

Rough inches across the widest point at grade — not the old trunk size. An estimate is fine.

How we get to it

Front yard, through a gate, or down a slope. If it's a gate, measure the opening — that decides which machine we bring.

What's going in that spot

Grass, concrete, a fence post, or a new tree. This changes how deep we need to go.

Anything buried nearby

Sprinkler heads, low-voltage lighting, invisible fence, septic. We probe, but you know your yard.
