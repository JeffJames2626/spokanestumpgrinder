Local rules

What Airway Heights actually requires

These are the city's written rules, not our house policy. A backyard stump and a street tree are different jobs.

Street and park stumps have to go below the surface

Airway Heights Municipal Code 12.34.120 says stumps of street and park trees shall be removed below the surface so the top does not project above the ground. The city also keeps the right to plant, prune, and remove trees in streets and public grounds, and to decide which trees belong in the right-of-way (12.34.080 and 12.34.110).

Limbs over a street need eight feet of clearance

Section 12.34.100 says the owner of a tree hanging over a street or sidewalk has to prune it so it doesn't block a streetlight or an intersection, with at least eight feet of clear space. The city gives two weeks' written notice before it comes onto private property for a dead or dangerous tree.

Right-of-way work needs a permit first

The public works department requires a permit before anyone grades, excavates, or otherwise disturbs a public right-of-way or easement. That is the planting strip and the highway frontage. It is not the middle of a fenced backyard.

A private-yard grind is usually not that permit

The city says landscaping, and tree or vegetation removal, needs a permit only if the excavation or fill passes the city's thresholds, or if the lot has a critical area such as a steep slope, wetland, or stream. The city page does not publish that threshold as a single number, so we don't invent one. If a stump looks like it sits in the right-of-way, we'll say so before we schedule. Questions on the threshold go to the city's planning department. Their page is here.

We file the 811 locate

Utilities get marked before the wheel turns. Spokane Stump Grinder submits the 811 request as part of scheduling. 811 does not mark private lines such as sprinklers. When those are in the way, we flag it. Read the 811 post and the private-locate post.

The work

Grind it, or grind it and leave the lawn ready

Airway Heights is on the same list as the rest of the Spokane and Coeur d'Alene region. Two services. No flat rate posted, because a stump against a new fence is not the same job as one in an open yard.

Stump grinding

The stump and the surface roots come down below grade. Chips go back in the hole and get tamped level. You can mow it. Most single stumps are a same-visit job. What's included.

Removal and cleanup

Everything in grinding, plus we haul the chips off and backfill with clean topsoil so the spot is finished level with the yard. This is the one to pick when something is going back in that spot.

Lawn repair after the tree

Renovating the lawn starts with the hole, not the seed. Chips left in the ground settle and sink. Cleanup hauls that wood out and leaves soil you can seed or sod over. We don't sell a separate lawn-renovation package, and we don't quote the grass.

What moves the number is on the services page: diameter at grade, root flare, depth, access, obstacles, what's in the wood, how many stumps, and whether the chips stay. Send a photo with the service request if you can.

Questions

Airway Heights stump questions

Do you take stump jobs in Airway Heights?

Yes. Airway Heights is on the service-area list with the rest of the West Plains towns we cover. Call or text 509-434-9554, or send the details through the request form.

Does my backyard stump need a city permit?

Usually the permit question is about the right-of-way, not the middle of a yard. Street and park stumps have to be taken below the surface, and disturbing the public right-of-way needs a public-works permit. On a private lot, the city requires a permit for tree removal when excavation passes its thresholds or the lot has a critical area. We will tell you if the stump looks like it sits in the strip along the street or the highway.

Can you leave the spot ready to seed or sod?

Yes, if you choose removal and cleanup. Grinding leaves the chips in the hole, raked and tamped. Cleanup hauls the chips and backfills with topsoil, finished level with the surrounding yard. That is the setup for repairing the lawn. We don't install the seed or the sod.

Who calls 811?

We do. The locate request is part of scheduling. You don't have to file it yourself. Private lines, including irrigation, are not part of an 811 locate.

Why won't you list a price for an Airway Heights stump?

Two stumps the same width can be an hour apart once you add the roots, the fence, the depth, and whether the machine can reach the backyard. You get a written estimate for your stump before we schedule. Nothing on this page is a rate.

Got a stump in Airway Heights?

Send a photo, a rough diameter, and a note about access. We'll answer with a written estimate.
